# 2026-11-23 — P2-6: own decoder vs `<video>` elements, 30 jump cuts

The tools and method are in `bench/p26/README.md`. **"Wrong picture"** means the frame on the canvas is not the one
the timeline asks for: neither the frame at the playhead nor the nearest frame, which is what an export renders.

Each run plays 13.5 s with 30 cuts. The page (canvas) refreshes about 31 times a second. The numbers are lab numbers
on a Mac (Safari 18.6 and headless Chromium), **not yet from a real iPad**.

What the `<video>` path does wrong in WebKit:

- About 25–30% of the pictures drawn are 2–3 frames off what the timeline asks for.
- The first run of a page often starts cuts on the wrong picture, and with effects it froze up to 380 ms.
- Every paused seek takes about half a second.

## Found and fixed while measuring (Safari)

1. **A Safari decoder keeps a few frames back until more input arrives.**
   - Symptom: the first frame after a jump took about 165 ms, because the decoder was only given more input after 150 ms.
   - Fix: how much extra input a file needs is remembered (`extraNeeded`). First frame p95 is now about 25 ms.
2. **Keeping more decoded frames than a hardware decoder has output pictures stalls it.**
   - Symptom: after fix 1, Safari reported "holds frames until a flush" 3 times and fell back to `<video>`.
   - Fix: the frames kept are capped separately from the extra input in flight.
3. **The last frames of each stretch stayed inside the decoder.**
   - Symptom: about 10 freezes of 100 ms per run, always just before a cut.
   - Fix: feeding goes past the stretch's end by that same extra input. Freezes went to 0.

Each fix has a unit test in `test/framesource.test.js` that fails without it.
